## Questions to Reflect On

1. In the last few months, where did I feel most able to speak honestly and without pressure?
2. What situations helped me feel more comfortable expressing my real thoughts?
3. When did I notice myself holding back, and what was the reason?
4. What helped me become more open in conversations with others?
5. Which discussion made me feel most confident in sharing my opinion?<!--more-->
6. How did I respond when I disagreed with someone respectfully?
7. In what moments did I feel heard and understood?
8. What changes in my communication made it easier to express myself clearly?
9. How did I handle a conversation where I wanted to say more but chose to pause?
10. What did I learn about my own communication style during this course?
11. Where did I feel safe enough to ask a question or share a concern?
12. What made it easier for me to be authentic in group settings?
13. How have my conversations changed in the last few months?
14. Which experience showed me that my voice matters in a meaningful way?
15. What would I like to keep improving so I can express myself more freely in future situations?
